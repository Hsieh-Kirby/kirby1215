<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">今天看一部網路節目，是有關</span><span lang="EN-US">DV(</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">夫妻間暴力</span><span lang="EN-US">)</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">的特集。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">其中，我對支援</span><span lang="EN-US">DV</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">被害者的</span><span lang="EN-US">NPO</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">代表說的話留下很深的印象。</span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「會做出</span><span lang="EN-US">DV</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">的人，是把對方當成自己的所有物。</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">因為這樣</span><span lang="JA" style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">過於期待，對方沒有照自己的想法做時便生氣，施行暴力」</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">原來如此。除了心有所感，也想到，這件事情不是別人的事。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">很理所當然，我們對於親近的人，看作是</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">我的</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">」妻子、「我的」部下、「我的」後輩。因為這樣，</span><span face="細明體" style="mso-bidi-font-family: 細明體;">曾幾何時自己的腦中便把這個人塑造成理想的模樣，過度期待，當對方做出不同的言行時，便不滿足，便生氣。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">天理教教導</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">這個世界是神的身驅</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">」。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;;"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">只有心是自己的東西，除此之外，沒有任何東西是自己的。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">遭遇的事情，遇到的人，得到的東西，全部都包含</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「讓人過</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">康樂生活</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">」的父母神的溫暖父母心。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;;"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">因此，無必要的事物及相遇，在這世上根本不存在。</span><span lang="EN-US">(</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">所有事物及相遇都是必要的</span><span lang="EN-US">)<o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">還有，天理教也教導如心所現的世界</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;">(</span><span lang="JA" style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">心通りの世界</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;">)</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">不是依願望，也不是依據想法，是依據內心。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">遭遇的事情，遇到的人，都反映出自己的內心。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">辛苦的事，難受的事，討厭的事，難過的事，惱人的事。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">當發生這些事情時馬上就指向對方，認為是某某人造成的，但重要的是，指的方向要轉向自己。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">把</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">我的東西」修正為「我的事情」。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;;"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">一年前，新冠開始蔓延，當活動一個接著一個被迫中止，在此狀況下，我最先感覺到的是</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「把一直舉辦到現在的事情，</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">轉換為信仰</span><span style="font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;; mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">」。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-font-family: &quot;MS Mincho&quot;;"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">信仰其實是日常的事情，一直把人聚集、企畫活動，時間和勞力被分割，實踐教導反而被放在後面去了。我深刻反省。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">並不是所有的活動都否定。但是，我深感應該要再一次重返教祖的教導，將日常生活與教導做結合。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">父母神賜與生命的每一天，若是什麼事都沒去思考就渡過實在很可惜。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="font-size: large;"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">珍惜父母神為了實現康樂生活安排好的事物、人、東西、以及一切，讓父母神因我們的所做所為而微笑。</span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;"><span style="font-size: large;">明天，也一起實現康樂生活！</span></span><span lang="EN-US"><o:p></o:p></span></p>--<p></p><p>(原文)</p><p></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">「我がものから我がことに」</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;"><o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">今日、あるネット番組を見ていると、</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;">DV</span><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">（夫婦間における暴力）のことについて特集されていた。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;"><o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span face="&quot;新細明體&quot;,&quot;serif&quot;" lang="JA" style="mso-ascii-font-family: Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font: minor-latin; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-language: JA; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-hansi-font-family: Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font: minor-latin;">当然のことながら、僕たちは近しい人にほど、“私の”妻、“私の”部下、”私の”後輩、というように考える。だからこそ、いつの間にか自らの頭の中でその人の理想像を作り上げ、過度な期待をし、相手がそれとは違った言動をしたときに不足したり、腹を立てたりしてしまうのだ。</span><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-fareast-language: JA;"><o:p></o:p></span></p>
